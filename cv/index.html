CV summary

My training combines pharmacy, bioinformatics, chemistry, and machine learning for computer-aided drug discovery. I am currently an Erasmus Mundus double-degree master's student in Bioinformatics at University Paris Cite and Chemistry at the University of Milan.

I previously completed a Bachelor of Pharmacy at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City. My thesis focused on knowledge-guided graph self-supervised learning for molecular property prediction.
