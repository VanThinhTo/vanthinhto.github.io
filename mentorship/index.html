Mentorship

My mentorship style is project-centered. I prefer focused collaborations around real research questions, reproducible workflows, and tangible outputs such as software, benchmark datasets, manuscripts, or conference abstracts.

One-person mentorship track

Availability: one student or early-career collaborator at a time.

Focus: cheminformatics, QSAR modeling, virtual screening, docking, molecular representation learning, and generative models for drug discovery.

Support: project framing, literature orientation, reproducible coding practice, evaluation design, documentation, and writing.
