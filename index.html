About

I am a double-degree master's student in Bioinformatics at University Paris Cite and Chemistry at the University of Milan. I previously completed a five-year Bachelor of Pharmacy at the University of Medicine and Pharmacy at Ho Chi Minh City, where my thesis focused on knowledge-guided graph self-supervised learning for molecular property prediction.

My current work includes automated retrieval of bioactivity data from PubChem and ChEMBL, dark chemical matter selection, benchmarking data sets for virtual screening, and target-specific scoring functions for drug discovery.

Research Interests

Molecular Representation

Knowledge-guided graph learning, orbital information, and molecular hierarchy.

Virtual Screening

Target-specific scoring, curated bioactivity data, and benchmarking workflows.

Generative Molecules

Evaluation and optimization of generated molecules for target-specific activity.
