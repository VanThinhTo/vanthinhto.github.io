This ongoing research effort builds benchmarking data sets for machine-learning
and docking workflows by automating bioactivity retrieval from PubChem and
ChEMBL.

Current directions:

- Large-scale target-centric curation pipelines.
- Dark chemical matter identification for assumed-inactive compounds.
- Benchmark design for reproducible virtual screening studies.
- Foundation work for target-specific scoring functions.
