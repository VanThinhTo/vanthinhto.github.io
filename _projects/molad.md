MolAD focuses on a practical question in cheminformatics: when should a model
prediction be trusted? The project packages applicability-domain methods for
small-molecule modeling and screening.

Methods included:

- PCA with confidence ellipse analysis.
- PCA with bounding-box style decision regions.
- Similarity-driven convex-hull approaches for out-of-domain detection.
