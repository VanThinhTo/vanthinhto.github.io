KGG is a molecular representation learning project that injects chemical
knowledge into self-supervised graph learning. The work combines hierarchical
molecular graphs, defragmentation, and orbital-information guidance to improve
property prediction across benchmark tasks.

Key outcomes:

- Better downstream classification and regression performance than strong
  MoleculeNet baselines.
- A cleaner framing for combining domain knowledge with graph representation
  learning.
- A direct bridge between thesis work and later publication in
  [JCIM](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c01068).
