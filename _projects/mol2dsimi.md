Mol2DSimi is a workflow for comparing molecular fingerprints and validating
ligand-based virtual screening performance with similarity metrics and
enrichment analysis.

Core features:

- Calculates multiple molecular fingerprints and similarity scores.
- Benchmarks screening quality with ROC-AUC, enrichment factors, F1, and GH.
- Supports fingerprint comparison and post-hoc statistical analysis for model
  selection.
